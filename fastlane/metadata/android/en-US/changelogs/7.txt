USB devices: Moondrop DACs such as the Dawn Pro now show up in the app.
- Plugging in a Moondrop DAC offers to open OpenDrop, which gets USB access on the spot
- USB devices are listed even when Bluetooth is off or the earbuds aren't connected
- Each DAC screen shows the formats it accepts, and the sample rates and bit depths Android can send it
- Developer options: set the Dawn Pro's filter and LED, with every change read back from the DAC
